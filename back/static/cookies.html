1. In short

  • By default we use only what is strictly necessary for the Service to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your language and interface settings, protecting payments and recording your cookie choice.
  • One optional item: Sign in with Google. It is used only after you allow it.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no social media pixels, no cross-site tracking, no profiling, and we never sell data.
  • Refusing costs you nothing. Every part of the Service stays available, and you can always sign in with your email or phone and password.
  • Fonts are served from our own servers, so no font provider receives your IP address when you open our pages.

2. The rules we follow

Storing information on your device, or accessing information already stored there, is allowed without consent only where it is strictly necessary to provide a service you have explicitly requested — Section 89(3) of Act No. 127/2005 Coll., on Electronic Communications, and Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC. Anything else requires your prior consent that is freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous within the meaning of Articles 4(11) and 7 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Pre-ticked boxes, silence, continued browsing or scrolling are never treated as consent. Personal data obtained this way is processed as described in the Privacy Policy.

3. Your choice, and how to change it

  • On your first visit a notice lets you Accept all or Reject all — each is a single click and both are equally prominent — or choose per category under Settings. Until you decide, nothing optional is used, and the Service works normally in the meantime. Closing or ignoring the notice is not consent.
  • If you have not allowed Sign in with Google and press Continue with Google on the sign-in or registration page, that click — made with the explanation shown directly below the button — is your consent to Sign in with Google only. Google's own button then appears, and you complete the sign-in with a second click.
  • You can change or withdraw your choice at any time, as easily as you gave it: under Cookie settings in the footer of our website and of the sign-in pages, or in Profile inside the CRM. Withdrawal takes effect immediately in that browser and does not affect the lawfulness of anything done before it.
  • Your decision — whether you accepted or refused — is kept for 6 months. After that, or if we introduce a new purpose that needs consent, we ask again. We do not ask again sooner after a refusal.
  • Your choice is stored only in your browser, together with the date, the policy revision and a random identifier that is not linked to your account, so that the choice can be demonstrated. Choices apply per browser and per device.
  • You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you block the strictly necessary items, signing in will not work.
