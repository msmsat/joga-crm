1. Our two roles — read this first

Velora handles personal data in two different capacities, and your rights differ depending on which applies:

  • As processor — for everything a studio enters into the CRM about its own clients and staff (bookings, memberships, payments, notes, messages). There the studio is the controller: it decides what to collect and why, and it answers requests from its clients. We act only on the studio's instructions under Annex A. If you are a client of a studio and want your data removed, contact that studio; we will forward any request we receive to it.
  • As controller — for the studio's own account with us: registration details, billing and invoicing data, records of acceptance of our Terms, security logs and support correspondence. There we decide the purposes ourselves and this policy governs directly.

3. Instagram integration

When a studio connects an Instagram professional account, we receive an access token issued by Meta. It is encrypted before it is written to our database, so a copy of the database alone does not expose it; it is never shown in the interface or in logs, and it is used only to read incoming direct messages and send replies. The connection can be revoked at any time from the Velora AI page of the CRM, or from Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Revoking it deletes the stored token immediately.

6. Cookies

We use only what is technically necessary to keep you signed in and to run the Service. We use no advertising cookies, no analytics and no third-party tracking, so no cookie consent banner is shown and none is required under Section 89(3) of the Czech Electronic Communications Act.

7. Security

Data travels over TLS and is stored on access-restricted servers with storage-level encryption at rest. Access tokens for connected messaging channels are encrypted a second time by the application before being written to the database. Passwords are kept only as salted one-way hashes and API keys only as hashes — neither can be read back by us. Access inside the Service is limited by role and enforced on the server. Card numbers never reach our systems; they are held by our payment provider. The database is backed up automatically every day and backups are kept for 14 days, so data can be restored after an incident. The full list of measures is in section A.5 of Annex A. If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the affected studio without undue delay and, where required, the supervisory authority under Article 33 GDPR.
