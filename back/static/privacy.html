Privacy Policy

Velora CRM · Last updated: 27 July 2026

Velora CRM ("Velora", "we") is a business management platform for fitness, yoga and wellness studios. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can have it removed.

1. Who controls your data

Each studio using Velora is the controller of its own business and client data. Velora acts as the processor: we store and process that data on the studio's behalf and only to provide the service.

2. What we collect

  • Studio account data: name, email address, phone number, business name, address and working hours.
  • Client records entered by the studio: name, contact details, date of birth, visit history, memberships, payments and notes.
  • Instagram data (only if the studio connects its Instagram professional account): the account's Instagram user ID and username, and the content of direct messages sent to that account, together with the sender's Instagram-scoped ID. This is used solely to deliver automated replies on the studio's behalf.
  • Technical data: log records, IP address and timestamps needed for security and troubleshooting.

3. Why we process it

To operate the service: scheduling, client management, payments, notifications and automated replies to messages received by a connected Instagram account. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Instagram integration

When a studio connects an Instagram professional account, we receive an access token issued by Meta. It is stored encrypted at rest and used only to read incoming direct messages and send replies. The connection can be revoked at any time from the Velora AI page of the CRM, or from Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Revoking it deletes the stored token immediately.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with infrastructure providers necessary to run the service (hosting, payment processing, email delivery) and with Meta Platforms when the studio uses the Instagram integration. We disclose data to authorities only where legally required.

6. Retention

Business and client data is kept while the studio's account is active. After the account is closed the data is deleted within 90 days, except records we must retain for accounting or legal reasons.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing smat5302@gmail.com with the subject "Data request". We respond within 30 days.

To delete data associated with an Instagram account, disconnect the account in the CRM — the stored token and Instagram identifiers are removed at once — or send a deletion request to the address above.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over TLS and stored on access-restricted servers. Access tokens and passwords are never stored in plain text.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The revision date at the top always reflects the current version.

10. Contact
