1. Our two roles — read this first

Velora handles personal data in two different capacities, and your rights differ depending on which applies:

  • As processor — for everything a studio enters into the CRM about its own clients and staff (bookings, memberships, payments, ratings, notes, messages, AI conversations). There the studio is the controller: it decides what to collect and why, and it answers requests from its clients. We act only on the studio's instructions under Annex A. If you are a client of a studio and want your data removed, contact that studio; we will forward any request we receive to it.
  • As controller — for the studio's own account with us: registration details, billing and invoicing data, records of acceptance of our Terms, security logs and support correspondence, and notices of illegal content sent to us. There we decide the purposes ourselves and this policy governs directly.

3. Instagram integration

When a studio connects an Instagram professional account, we receive an access token issued by Meta. It is encrypted before it is written to our database, so a copy of the database alone does not expose it; it is never shown in the interface or in logs, and it is used only to read incoming direct messages and send replies. The connection can be revoked at any time from the Velora AI page of the CRM, or from Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Revoking it deletes the stored token immediately.

4. Artificial intelligence

  • What it does. Velora AI answers questions of a studio's staff about their business, summarises data and drafts texts. Where a studio enables it, an AI agent also answers the studio's clients in Telegram, Instagram or WhatsApp — about schedules, prices and, for a recognised client, their own bookings and membership.
  • What is sent, and to whom. To answer, the question and the data needed for it are sent to OpenRouter, Inc. (USA), which passes them to one of the AI model providers listed in Annex B of the Terms (Google, Anthropic or OpenAI). The assistant sees only the data of the studio's own account, limited by the role of the person asking; an AI agent talking to a client sees only that client's own bookings and the studio's public information.
  • No training. Every request is sent with collection of data for training disabled, so providers that keep prompts for training are excluded. We do not train AI models on your data ourselves.
  • You are told when it is AI. A studio's AI agent introduces itself as an AI assistant of the studio, in line with Article 50 of the EU AI Act. If you would rather talk to a person, ask the agent or contact the studio directly.
  • AI can be wrong. Answers are generated automatically and may contain mistakes. Nothing an AI agent says about prices, places, discounts or refunds replaces the confirmation of the studio itself.
  • No automated decisions about you. The AI features do not make decisions that produce legal effects concerning a person or similarly significantly affect them (Article 22 GDPR). An AI agent cannot book, cancel, charge or refund anything; changes proposed by Velora AI are carried out only after a member of staff confirms them.
  • Quality. To keep the AI features working properly, we review ratings staff give to answers and requests the AI failed to handle. Access is limited to the provider, and the results are used only to improve the Service.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Velora stores in your browser only what the Service needs in order to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your language and interface settings, protecting payments and recording your cookie choice. These items are strictly necessary and need no consent under Section 89(3) of the Czech Electronic Communications Act (Act No. 127/2005 Coll.) and Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC.

Anything beyond that is used only with your prior consent. Today this is a single item — Sign in with Google, which loads Google's service and lets Google set its own cookies. We use no analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and fonts are served from our own servers. You can accept, refuse or withdraw consent at any time under "Cookie settings" in the footer of our pages or in your Profile in the CRM; refusing does not limit access to the Service.

Every item, with its provider, purpose and lifetime, is listed in the Cookie Policy.

8. Security

Data travels over TLS and is stored on access-restricted servers with storage-level encryption at rest. Access tokens for connected messaging channels are encrypted a second time by the application before being written to the database. Passwords are kept only as salted one-way hashes and API keys only as hashes — neither can be read back by us. Access inside the Service, including what the AI features can see, is limited by role and enforced on the server. Card numbers never reach our systems; they are held by our payment provider. The database is backed up automatically every day and backups are kept for 14 days, so data can be restored after an incident. The full list of measures is in section A.5 of Annex A. If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the affected studio without undue delay and, where required, the supervisory authority under Article 33 GDPR.
